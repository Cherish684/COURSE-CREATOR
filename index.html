❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Course Creator AI

What is Course Creator AI? ▼
General Course Creator AI is an AI-powered learning platform that generates complete 7-module courses on any topic you enter. Powered by Groq's LLaMA 3.3 model, it produces structured content with learning objectives, in-depth explanations, and key takeaways — all in seconds. You can also search YouTube videos to get AI-generated transcripts and quizzes, track your focus while studying, and earn points through difficulty-based quizzes.
How does the AI generate courses? ▼
Technology When you type a topic and click Generate, your request is sent to our Flask backend which constructs a detailed prompt and calls the Groq API (LLaMA 3.3 70B model). The AI acts as a combined researcher, evaluator, and course writer — returning a structured JSON object with 7 modules, each containing learning objectives, 300+ words of content, key takeaways, and a difficulty level. Module images are generated with Cloudflare Workers AI (FLUX.1 schnell).
How does the Video Course feature work? ▼
Features Search any topic to find YouTube videos. Once you select a video and click Generate, the backend downloads the audio using yt-dlp, uploads it to AssemblyAI for transcription, then splits the transcript into 400-word chunks stored in a SQLite FTS5 database. The most relevant chunks are retrieved using full-text search and passed to Groq to generate a 10-question quiz. Transcripts are cached — so the same video is never re-processed.
Do I need to create an account? ▼
Account No — you can use the platform as a guest by clicking "Skip for now" on the landing page. However, creating a free account lets you save courses to the database, access your course history, set a preferred language for course generation, submit and view feedback, and have your last generated course automatically restored when you return.
What is the Focus Detector? ▼
Features The Focus Detector uses your device camera and a face detection model to check whether you are looking at the screen while studying. If you look away for too long, a gentle nudge appears to bring your attention back. Each study session records your total time, distracted time, and a focus score (0–100%). All session data is available in the Focus Report section and saved per user.
How does the points and levels system work? ▼
Gamification Every correct quiz answer earns you points based on difficulty — Easy gives 5 pts, Medium gives 10 pts, and Hard gives 15 pts. Points are tracked separately per difficulty level. Each level has a completion threshold: Easy completes at 500 pts, Medium at 1000 pts, and Hard at 1500 pts. All levels are unlocked from the start so you can attempt any difficulty at any time. Your progress is saved locally in your browser.
Can I generate courses in languages other than English? ▼
Languages Yes. Go to Feedback → Settings and select your preferred language from the dropdown. Supported languages include Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean. Once set, all generated courses will be written entirely in your chosen language. Your preference is saved to your account if you are logged in.
What is the Upload File feature? ▼
Features You can upload images (JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF) or PDF documents and ask the AI questions about them. The files are sent to Groq's vision model (Llama 4 Scout) which reads diagrams, handwritten notes, textbook pages, or any visual content and gives you a detailed answer. You can have a full multi-turn conversation with follow-up questions about the same uploaded files.
Is my data stored securely? ▼
Privacy User accounts, courses, feedback, quiz attempts, and engagement sessions are stored in a local SQLite database on the server. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt before storage — they are never stored in plain text. Video transcripts are cached in a separate SQLite FTS5 database. Your quiz points and theme preference are stored in your browser's localStorage and never sent to the server.
Why does video processing take a few minutes? ▼
Performance The first time you process a video, the backend downloads its audio (yt-dlp), uploads it to AssemblyAI, waits for transcription to complete, then runs the quiz generation through Groq. This pipeline can take 1–3 minutes depending on video length and API response times. However, once processed, the transcript is permanently cached in SQLite — so if you or any user selects the same video again, the quiz generates almost instantly without any re-downloading or re-transcription.
